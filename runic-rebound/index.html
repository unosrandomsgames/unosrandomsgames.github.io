English

Year 2099. A rogue AI sealed the megacity of NEO-RUNA under giant runic seals. Only one spark remains: you.

  • Aim and launch a volley of light balls that bounce between the walls.
  • Engrave runes on the walls: fire, frost, lightning, echo… and combine them.
  • Defeat procedural bosses and hack their core in 15 cyberpunk minigames.
  • Every boss takes you to a new sector of the city.

Free with optional ads. English and Spanish.
