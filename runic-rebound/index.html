How a run works

1

Aim and launch

Fire a volley of light balls and bank them off the walls to shatter the seals before they reach the bottom.

2

Engrave runes

Pick a rune and choose which wall segment to carve it into. Balls gain its power only when they bounce there.

3

Hack the boss

Defeat procedural bosses, then crack their core in a cyberpunk minigame to earn a new ability.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free and shows ads. Video ads are always optional and give you a reward. There are no in-app purchases.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Ads and rewarded videos need a connection, but the game itself works offline.

Which languages?

English and Spanish.

When does it launch?
