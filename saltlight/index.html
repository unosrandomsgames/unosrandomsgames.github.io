Saltlight

Faro de Sal in Spanish

Defend the lighthouse, forge weapons and lead your village against the grey tide.

Coming soon iOS Android

A salt-flat coast, a stone lighthouse and a grey tide that rises every night. You are the lightkeeper: if the light goes out, the village falls.

How to play

Every night, four steps

1

Spend the salt at low tide

Build nets, harpoon posts, cauldrons and bells, and upgrade them from wood to iron and bronze.

2

Assign your villagers

Every villager is either income or defense, never both: you choose who gathers salt and who wields a harpoon.

3

Fight the wave

Move your hero with one thumb, strike in an arc with one button and use your skills at the right moment.

4

Beat the boss and set sail

Every island ends with a boss. Win and sail on to the next island of the archipelago.
